Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

How trophy data is handled

YLTA — Your Local Trophy Analyzer runs in your browser. Trophies and preferences you enter or import are saved in your browser using IndexedDB, with localStorage as a fallback. The website does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization.

Goals and checklist data

Goals, descriptions, optional game associations, deadlines, checklist items, and completion statuses are saved locally in the same browser storage as your trophies. Goals may use checklists or update automatically from your imported collection. Goal data is not sent to the import server. You can export goals together with your trophies in a YLTA JSON backup.

Saved PSNProfiles pages

When you select saved PSNProfiles HTML pages, YLTA parses their trophy names and rarity percentages directly in your browser to match them against your local PSN trophy collection. YLTA does not upload those saved HTML files or their extracted percentages to its own server. No PSNProfiles account password is required.

Optional PSNProfiles browser helper

The optional, user-installed YLTA browser helper reads trophy pages you already have open only when you click its capture buttons. It stores trophy names, rarity percentages and source page URLs in your browser extension’s local storage until you clear them, and exports a JSON file on your device. It does not automatically fetch webpages, send collected data to YLTA servers or request your PSNProfiles account credentials.

PlayStation account access

YourLTA does not ask for your PlayStation password. The optional phone/web import does request an NPSSO credential and sends it to the YourLTA-operated import server on Render to retrieve trophies from PSN. The alternative Windows exporter runs locally and communicates with Sony through a third-party software library. JSON files selected for import are processed in your browser. Both PSN access methods are unofficial. Treat NPSSO as a sensitive credential and do not post or send it in feedback.

Changing domains and backups

Browser storage is isolated by website address. Moving to another URL does not copy your trophies, goals, or preferences automatically. Export a JSON backup on the old site and import it into the new site before deleting anything.

Third-party fonts

YLTA loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google servers when loading the font files, which can disclose technical connection information. No trophy data is deliberately included in those requests.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and advertising

This version of YLTA — Your Local Trophy Analyzer does not include advertising or third-party visitor analytics scripts. If these are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

Managing your data

You can download a JSON backup containing your trophies and goals. You can also delete individual goals and checklist steps in the Goals tab. Clearing your browser's site data can remove locally stored trophies, goals, and preferences. Back up important data before clearing browser storage or changing website domains.

Beta feedback

The feedback page prepares a text report on your device. It does not submit reports or upload files. Share the report with the creator in the discussion where you found YourLTA. Do not include credentials or private account information.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the site operator through a contact method published on the website when available.

Mobile PSN import

If you choose mobile PSN import, your NPSSO credential is transmitted over HTTPS to the YourLTA-operated import server hosted on Render to retrieve trophy data. The application is not affiliated with Sony. The server code does not intentionally persist NPSSO credentials or log request bodies, but hosting infrastructure and account compromise remain risks. The token can authorize access beyond trophy viewing. Using unofficial PSN access may be restricted by Sony. Do not share tokens with anyone. You may use the offline JSON import option instead.
